# 🌸 For Your Fav Person 🤍

> *A very important celebration website dedicated to an absolute legend who finally accomplished something monumental (even if it just meant doing the bare minimum after procrastinating for three business days).* ✨🎉

---

## 🎀 About This Project

This is a personalized, interactive, and lovingly handcrafted celebration website made just for your best friend or study/life partner. 

Since your person just achieved a totally unhinged or surprisingly productive milestone, this little website was built to commemorate the event with the exact right balance of soft aesthetics, dramatic humor, and genuine best-friend encouragement.

---

## ✨ Features & Pages

*   **💌 The Opening Envelope:** A cute interactive envelope that opens with floating petals and sparkles to reveal the shocking news.
*   **⏱️ The Historic Event:** Breaks down their monumental achievement (complete with zero distractions and zero existential rabbit holes!).
*   **🏆 Achievement Dashboard & Badge:** Officially inducts your friend into the hall of fame for their specific victory.
*   **📜 Official Certificate:** A fake official certificate of extreme effort, complete with a custom goofy seal.
*   **💬 Heartfelt Real Talk:** Reminding them that starting is what truly matters.
*   **📈 The Progress Chart:** A milestone tracker capturing every phase of their journey from minute 5 to the final victory.
*   **🤝 Partners & Besties:** Celebrating the chaotic journey of surviving life/academics together (plus Polaroid placeholders!).
*   **🌼 The Hype Jar:** An interactive glass jar loaded with random notes of encouragement you can click anytime.
*   **☕ Cozy Break & Chat Corner:** Gives them a well-deserved breather with random funny questions before they get back to whatever they were doing.
*   **🫶 Final Peaceful Screen:** A warm, comforting wrap-up with a toggleable ambient music option.

---

## 🎨 Visual Aesthetic

*   **Color Palette:** Soft blush pink (`#fce4ec`), warm cream (`#fff9f0`), white, very light sage green (`#e2ede4`), and subtle gold accents (`#e6c594`).
*   **Details:** Delicate jasmine flowers, subtle paper textures, rounded cards, soft shadows, and handwritten typography.

---

## 🚀 How to Run

1. Clone or download this repository.
2. Open `index.html` and replace all instances of "Narabiiii" with your friend's name (and tweak the milestone text to fit what they actually did!).
3. Open the file in any modern web browser.
4. Send the link to your person and prepare the confetti! 🎉
